About this app

Victim Care {{.Version}} — built {{.Year}}.

Victim Care keeps emergency guidance, your emergency contact and a one tap alert in a single place. It is a companion, not a replacement for professional help.

Victim Care does not replace emergency services. If you are in immediate danger, call your local emergency number now.

How your data is used

  • Your password is hashed with bcrypt and is never stored in plain text.
  • Your pictures are stored on this server under a random file name.
  • Your location stays on your device until you choose to share it in a message.
  • No third party advertising, no selling of data, no third party analytics.

Credits

  • First-aid guidance reviewed by a certified first responder.
  • Icons drawn in-house, type set in Nunito.
  • Built with the Go standard library and SQLite.
