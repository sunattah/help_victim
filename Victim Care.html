First aid

Simple steps while you wait for help.

Heavy bleeding
  1. Press firmly on the wound with a clean cloth.
  2. Keep pressing. Do not lift the cloth to check.
  3. Raise the injured limb if you can.
  4. Call for help now.
Burns
  1. Cool the burn under running water for 20 minutes.
  2. Remove rings or tight items near the burn.
  3. Cover loosely with a clean non-stick cover.
  4. Do not use ice, butter or toothpaste.
Choking
  1. Ask them to cough. If they cannot, give 5 firm back blows.
  2. Give 5 abdominal thrusts.
  3. Repeat until the object comes out or help arrives.
Not breathing
  1. Call for help and ask for a defibrillator.
  2. Push hard and fast in the centre of the chest, about twice per second.
  3. Keep going until help takes over.
Broken bone
  1. Keep the person still.
  2. Support the injured part in the position found.
  3. Do not try to straighten it.
  4. Get medical help.
